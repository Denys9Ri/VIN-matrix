## Vehicle model catalog

Vehicle data by [VehiclesDB](https://vehiclesdb.com), version 2026.08.2,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
VIN-matrix selects the car, van, and truck catalogs, maps source makes to the
application make catalog, and deduplicates and sorts model names.

The VehiclesDB data is reconciled from the following official sources. The
source notices below are preserved as required by their respective licenses.
